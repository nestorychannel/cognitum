# Cognitum: Scientific Dissemination Ecosystem

**Cognitum** is the multidisciplinary knowledge platform hosted by **Nestory Channel**, dedicated to the scientific exploration of the intersection between **Body, Mind, and Spirit**. 

Led by **NA León Restrepo (MSc)**, this project operates under the *Independent Research Initiative* framework, ensuring scientific communication is accessible, transparent, and rigorous.

## 🔬 Research Focus
Cognitum bridges the gap between complex multidisciplinary sciences and public understanding, focusing on:
- **Integrity & Literacy:** Promoting scientific accuracy in the algorithmic era.
- **Well-being:** Scientific analysis of human holistic health.
- **Scientific Communication:** Bridging the gap between academic research and public dissemination.

## 📂 Repository Structure
Our ecosystem provides full traceability for every episode, including:
- **Technical Papers (PDFs):** In 1-column and 2-column formats.
- **Visual Assets:** Infographics and presentation slides.
- **Multimedia:** Podcast and Video production files.
- **Data & Metadata:** Open access schemas (`schema.json`) and bibliographic records (`cite.bib`) for seamless integration with citation managers.

## 🎓 Citation & Research
Cognitum promotes open science principles. We encourage the use and citation of our work. Please refer to the `CITATION.cff` file in the root directory for standard citation formats compatible with Zotero, Mendeley, and BibTeX.
